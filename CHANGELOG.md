## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-17

First release of laravel-quebec-audit-log: who viewed, changed, exported or anonymized whose personal information (Quebec Law 25, ss. 3.1 and 10), hash-chained and append-only, with a chain check command. Part of the Quebec Law 25 suite for Laravel.
